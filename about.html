About

About This Project

The Buddenbrooks Reader is an interactive tool designed to assist German learners in reading Thomas Mann's classic novel, Buddenbrooks. This project aims to lower the barrier to entry for reading complex German literature by providing instant, contextual vocabulary support.

How the Vocabulary Works

Words highlighted in blue are part of the vocabulary database. When you hover over or click a word, you see its English and German definitions. Clicking also adds the word to your vocabulary set; clicking again removes it.

Contributing

Many definitions were not initially automatically captured by my workflow and you can help expand the vocabulary set. If you find a word that is not defined, you can use the Neues Wort beitragen (Contribute a New Word) form on the main page to submit a new definition. Please enter the word as it appears in the text (e.g. accusative "kleinen" rather than "klein"). All submissions are reviewed for accuracy before being added to the main vocabulary files. I aim to update vocabuarly daily.

Data and Technology

The text is from Project Gutenberg. The text was processed with NLTK and spaCy and initial definitions were programatically generated with Google Gemini's API. The vocabulary data is powered by Firebase Firestore, and the static JSON files used by the reader are automatically synced to this repository via a GitHub Action. This ensures the app is fast while keeping the vocabulary continuously updated.
